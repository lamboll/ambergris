A large dark vulture. Wears a top hat. Always carries a small empty cage beside them (we never find out what was in it). Stands at the GANGPLANK and checks bags. Has a rule: CLEAR BAGS ONLY. Never explains. Never changes the rule. Until the very end — when they step aside.
Key prop: the empty cage + the top hat. Both must be in the drawing.

HAWK
An enormous osprey. Gold eye. Lives up in the BOATGARDEN — the very top of the world. Comes down only in SUNNIGHT when the fire starts. Much larger than any other bird character. Fills the whole panel when they appear. Has been watching the whole story from above.
Must show the gold eye. Must show scale — HAWK should look 3× bigger than TRAST.

COMMUTERPIGEONS
Pigeons who cycle past on the esplanade below the jetty. Always on bikes. Always looking up. Three of them. In SUNNIGHT they see TRAST's face when the mask falls off — and stop dead on their bikes, frozen in fear.
The bikes are part of who they are. Imagine pigeons wearing cycling gear, riding old-fashioned bicycles.

SB / THE ABSENT ONE

Could be drawn as green threads/sparks on a black page. Or as tiny glowing particles. Or as the word VIABRO itself made of green dots.
