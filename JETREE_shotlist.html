STRUCTURE NOTE
The route is linear but loops. Read left-to-right like a jetty: you walk out over the water (APPROACH), reach the TRUNK, climb up, enter the BRANCHES. The story plays once forward, then the Möbius fold — SUNNIGHT rhymes with MOONNIGHT, the last image rhymes with the first. Panels marked ╌ are deliberate echoes.

VOICE-OVER NOTE
No VO is written here. The panels are designed so the same images read differently depending on the voice speaking over them. Old male VO = TRAST narrating memory. Young girl VO = O telling the story of how they came to be. Same words, same images, different film.

SILENT GRAMMAR RULES:
— No text cards. No speech bubbles. Everything via gesture, eyeline, and proximity.
— What a character holds = what they value. What falls = what is lost.
— Eyeline tells the relationship: who looks at whom, who looks away.
— The GULL nod in panel 20 = the emotional climax of the first half.
— O's laugh in panel 24 = the emotional climax of the second half.
— O looking at the camera in panel 27 = the only fourth-wall break in the film.

VIABRO / B&W MODE:
In panels 01 and 30 (the Möbius pair), B&W mode reveals the VIABRO threads. In all other panels, VIABRO is invisible in colour mode. The audience only sees the full picture if they switch modes — or if they watch the film twice and recognise what was always there.
