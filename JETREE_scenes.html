The JETREE Scene List — 12 Scenes (4 times × 3 paths)

Reading order: MOONNIGHT → MOONDAY → SUNDAY → SUNNIGHT. Within each time: left branch → trunk → right branch. Descend the trunk between times = time jump.

░░ MOONNIGHT ░░

SB is recently dead. STICKINSECT has just reached imago state. VIABRO 1st generation — in bark/bone. No signal on TV.

Left Branch
— Approach

I arrives by TWIGBIKE from the dark left branch path. STICKINSECT is newly awakened — imago stage, vision already disrupted by faint early VIABRO waves from far above. STICKINSECT follows at a distance. TWIGBIKE folds at the branch junction.

Trunk
— JETREE 01 (alone)

I stands at the JETREE trunk node, alone, holding something. GULLGANG is present on the platform but ignores I completely — no eye contact. A has not arrived. TWIGFERRY is nowhere on the horizon. This is the night before everything starts.

Tech note: the JETREE trunk here is smooth bark. VIABRO is visible in B&W mode as faint traceries on the bark surface.
Right Branch → Sea
— Descent / Undersea prelude

The camera (eye) follows the SEATRUNK downward into PITCHBLACK. Dead bird skeleton on the seafloor. BUBBLES form — VIABRO in its first generation, barely alive. A tiny DOT of light appears. MAMMA passes far above, a shadow.

░░ MOONDAY ░░

TWIGFERRY arrives. First meeting. Bottle/camera misunderstanding. Mask scene. 9/11 on TV. VIABRO 2nd generation — in glass/drops/chemical liquid.

Left Branch
— Ferry Approach

TWIGFERRY appears on the horizon after I has been waiting by the TWIGSIGN. Approaches slowly. GULLGANG launches from the JETREE to circle it. TICKETVULTURE stands at the GANGPLANK. A is on deck — BAGS, CAMERA, cigarette. The GANGPLANK descends.

Panel sizing: 10w × 7h — the big ferry/gangplank scene. TICKETVULTURE grey/black with top hat, cage in hand.
Trunk
— JETREE 01 (First Meet)

Scene A: I thinks A's BOTTLE is full of liquid drops (medicine — I uses drops for air, not drinking). A's bottle is actually broken in a bag. A has another at home. Misunderstanding: I reaches for the bottle; A is embarrassed. The CAMERA is what A really has. A peels back the panels of their eyes — transfers lens images in chemical trays to leaves. I is astonished.

Scene B: I flies into A's BOTTLE lens — literally fits inside (I is tiny, a Fairybird). A has to get I out carefully. GULLGANG watches. First laugh.

Scene C: MASK scene — after an attack on TRAST, TICKETVULTURE institutes a new rule: no bags without checking. A loses bags temporarily. I is furious.

GREENWREN appears — hummingbird colleague of I. A sees GREENWREN as pure grey pixel static. "I can read you real fast. You a liar." GREENWREN retreats.
Right Branch
— Cuttlefish Bones

A tries to take CUTTLEFISH BONES on board. TICKETVULTURE stops them. I kicks the bones toward the water thinking they're clearing the gangplank. But the bones were A's treasure — they were going to sell them (bones → sand → GLASS). The bones fall into the sea.

Slots in — undersea: BOTTLE also falls into water. BUBBLES escape, travel along PYLON, reach CUTTLEFISH BABY. CUTTLEFISH skin flashes PIXELETTERS. VIABRO multiplies.
░░ SUNDAY ░░

Relationship established. O is present (wheelchair). Ambergris is found. COVID on TV. VIABRO 3rd generation — in vellum/paper/writing.

Left Branch
— Vellum Delivery

I returns with a packet of VELLUM — proper vellum with flesh still stuck at the edges. Brings it inside the PERCHGARDEN. The VIABRO in the flesh makes the FLIES go wild. A is annoyed. STICKINSECT approaches. STICKINSECT's vision is disrupted by VIABRO waves from the TREETOP — seeing through walls/time.

I also brings the TWIGBIKE. A: "I wish you wouldn't bring that inside." I folds the spokes onto the stem — compresses to a small bundle.
Trunk
— JETREE (SKYMORN / DAY 21)

Scene A (SKYMORN): I and A's first actual real words — all earlier scenes were misunderstanding and gesture. FISHGULL sells SAND to a GULL on the jetty below (sand → glass). GULLGANG beginning to warm up. O is in the chair watching.

Scene B (DAY 21): I is writing (using CROWFEET from hat as dip pen). A is smoking, photographing the sea. O present in the wheelchair. GULLGANG comfortable — sitting on the platform rails. STICKINSECT in the background, observing.

Right Branch
— Ambergris Found

A dives with scuba tanks and bags. Descends through sea levels — VIABRO in the water column. CUTTO reaches out tentacle, touches A's wings — VIABRO jumps to A's feathers. A finds the AMBERGRIS in the deep. Surfaces. Holds it up. I leans over the JETREE platform edge.

The EGG: A brings up AMBERGRIS which has an EGG inside it — or transforms into the EGG as A rises. O recognises it and laughs. VIABRO glows in B&W mode. Slots in — domestic: A brings ambergris home. Smells it, tastes it, burns it, bathes in it. CHICK eats ambergris → SYMBIOTE = CURE. The ambergris is O's medicine.
░░ SUNNIGHT ░░

Fire on the BOATGARDEN. HAWK appears. DARKROOM time cut. Space launch / Facebook ambergris on TV. VIABRO 5th generation — in digital pixels / vape cloud / screens.

Left Branch
— Skylif3 / Phone call

I receives a phone call — NEWSREADER on the other end. The call is about something that happened in the world. I listens. A is watching on the old wooden TV with blown glassbubble. The NEWS is happening. COMMUTERPIGEONS cycle past on the esplanade below, glancing up.

Tech progression: I now uses a VAPE instead of cigarettes. The cloud = VIABRO as engineered aerosol. A is switching from film (fly-lens + darkroom) toward digital — but resists. The darkroom remains A's centre.
Trunk
— BOATGARDEN Fire + HAWK Transition

Fire on the BOATGARDEN (flat top of the double cube). GULLGANG panics and scatters. HAWK appears — huge, filling the frame, osprey eye glowing yellow. HAWK is connected to SB's footage (SB had a HOUSECAMERA).

A's MASK comes off accidentally in the fire chaos. COMMUTERPIGEONS see A's real face and are terrified. O laughs — O has always known.

GULLGANG — fully warm now, post-fire — carries O's wheelchair across the GANGPLANK as the TWIGFERRY prepares to leave. I helps from one side. A from the other. TICKETVULTURE steps aside without checking bags. First time.

HAWK speaks to I: "That's how we met your birddaddy" — told to O about FINK. HAWK carries this history.
Right Branch
— DARKROOM Time Cut

A enters the DARKROOM. Chemical trays. Empty bottles on shelves. The DARKROOM is the time machine: entering = going to the past, coming out = arriving in the future. TRAST develops photographs — each image = a moment that already happened. The DRAGONFLY cage is empty. STICKINSECT is nowhere. The MANTID has gone through its own transformation.

Final image: the JETREE from outside, at distance, night. The four faces of the double cube visible as four shades of dark. VIABRO visible in B&W mode — threads of green light connecting every node.

The I · A · O Answer

The rune dictionary (IMG_3370) reveals that ALGIZ (ᛉ) = the JETREE shape = "Highseekers / Find IAO / sacrifice."

I · A · O are the three mystic vowels of the Gnostic divine name. The three characters are not just a family — they are the three sounds of the name carved into the world-tree. The JETREE exists to help them find each other. VIABRO is what flows between the letters when the name is spoken.

The project is called AMBERGRIS — the whale's gut-product, the floating grey lump that smells terrible and is worth a fortune and is the host for VIABRO. The whole story is contained in that object: decay becomes treasure, the invisible becomes visible, the dead generate the living.
