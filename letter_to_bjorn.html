One is small and white. One is bigger and dark.

I'll take your drawings and turn them into game sprites and film characters. You'll be able to watch your own drawings move across a screen. The website will have your name on it. And when the film is finished, it will be shown as part of your MDAi program.

This is Bjorn's film.
Bjorn's game.
Bjorn's world.
